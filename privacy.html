PRIVACY POLICY

Muiller & Friends BBQ Scorecard · Last updated May 21, 2026

WHAT THIS APP DOES

The Muiller & Friends BBQ Scorecard is a personal restaurant review tool. It lets you score BBQ restaurants, track visits, and keep notes. All review data is stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage.

GOOGLE DRIVE ACCESS

The app offers an optional Google Drive sync feature. If you choose to connect, the app creates and updates a single JSON file in your Google Drive containing your review data. This uses the Google Drive API with the "drive.file" scope, which limits access to only files created by this app. The app cannot see, read, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.

WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

None. The app runs entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. We have no servers, no databases, no analytics, and no tracking. Your review data lives on your device and, if you opt in, in your own Google Drive.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The app uses Google OAuth to authenticate Drive sync and OpenStreetMap via Leaflet for the map view. No data is shared with these services beyond what is required for authentication and map tile loading.

COOKIES AND TRACKING

The app does not use cookies, analytics, or any form of user tracking. Theme preferences and review data are stored in your browser's localStorage and never leave your device.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
