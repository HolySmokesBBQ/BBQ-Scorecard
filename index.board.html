How BBQ Board works

Every price on BBQ Board has a store, a cut, a price per pound, and a timestamp. Sort by distance and price. Filter to just butcher shops if you want to support local — every Costco, Walmart, and Meijer drops out. Prices older than 30 days get flagged as stale; older than 90 hidden entirely. Anyone can submit a new price by signing in with Google, or by uploading a photo of a weekly ad for the app to OCR.
