THE APPS

BBQ SCORECARD

Rate every BBQ joint you visit across 10 competition-style categories. Appearance, smoke, tenderness, sauce, sides, and how the place treats families all get scored. If the food's bad, the score says so.

Launch Scorecard Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store

BBQ BOARD

Where’s the cheapest brisket in town? BBQ Board tracks meat prices from local butchers, grocery counters, and warehouse clubs. Community-updated. If the price shifts, submit the new one. Covering 100+ cities across 23 states.

Launch Board Get it on Google Play Coming soon to the App Store

BBQ NOTEBOOK

Free cook log for pitmasters. Log the rub, the wood, the temps, the weather, and what you'd change next time. Import cooks from 11 thermometer apps. Earn tiers as you cook, from Brisket Boss to Hall of the Yardbird. Private by default.

Launch Notebook Get it on Google Play

BBQ CALCULATOR

How much brisket for fifty people? Plug in the guests and pick your pit. Raw weight to buy, cook time, batches on your smoker, when to light the fire, and what to grab at the store. If you’re feeding twenty, you don’t need an app for that.
