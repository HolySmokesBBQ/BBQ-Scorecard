Why prices swing week to week

Wholesale beef and pork prices move on packing-plant supply, feed costs, seasonal demand (brisket spikes before Memorial Day, ribs before the 4th, whole turkey in November), and store-level clearance decisions. A brisket at $5.89/lb at Costco on Tuesday can be $7.99 by Saturday if the pallet moves. Board's job is to make the current week's numbers visible before you drive across town.

Which cuts to check for ${CITY_NAME} BBQ

  • Choice packer brisket: the workhorse for smoked brisket. Look for $5.99–$8.99/lb depending on shop.
  • Boston butt / pork shoulder: usually the cheapest smoker cut per pound. $2.49–$4.99/lb range.
  • Spare ribs (whole slab): cheaper than baby backs and better for competition-style. $3.99–$6.99/lb.
  • Whole chicken: often on sale under $2/lb; the best low-effort smoker cook for beginners.
  • Bratwurst (fresh): regional specialty in the Upper Midwest; check independent butchers first.
