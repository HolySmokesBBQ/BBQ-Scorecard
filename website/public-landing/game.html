HOLD 225

Keep the pit needle in the band for a 75-second cook. One tap feeds the fire.

The game runs in your browser and needs JavaScript turned on.

HOW IT PLAYS

A tap feeds the fire after a short lag, so tap a beat before the needle leaves the band.

Wind and an open lid pull the heat down. A few quick taps bring it back. When the lid shuts the fire climbs on its own for a second, so ease off before it does.

A flare pushes the heat up. Stop tapping until it passes.

The band tightens as the cook goes on, and a long stretch in band is worth more.

The cook ends at 1:15, or sooner if the fire goes out or the pit runs too hot.

On a keyboard, Tab to the start button and press Enter. Focus moves to the Feed the fire button, and Space feeds it. Escape pauses from anywhere on the page. P pauses while the game has focus, and R starts a new cook from the end card.

Your best score is kept in this browser.

The temperatures here are game numbers, not a cooking guide.
