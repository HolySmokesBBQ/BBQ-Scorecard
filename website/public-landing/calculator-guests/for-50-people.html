The quick answer for 50 people

Brisket for 50 people: about 50 lb raw
4 pencil-cut slices per person. That's roughly 5 packer briskets at a typical 12 lb each. Buy raw weight; smoking loses 50% to shrinkage.
Pulled Pork for 50 people: about 24 lb raw
5 oz per person (a solid sandwich). That's roughly 4 pork butts at a typical 8 lb each. Buy raw weight; smoking loses 35% to shrinkage.
St. Louis Ribs for 50 people: about 32 lb raw
3 ribs per person. That's roughly 11 racks at a typical 3 lb each. Buy raw weight; smoking loses 30% to shrinkage.
