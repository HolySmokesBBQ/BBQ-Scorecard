# ASO audit: BBQ Scorecard, both stores (2026-10-23)

This is the first run of the `aso` skill (Corey Haines marketingskills pack).
It reads the live listings: the App Store through the iTunes lookup API plus
the public page, and Google Play through the public page. Scoring uses the
skill's rubric at the **Challenger** tier (under 100K ratings), which scores
strictly against textbook ASO.

Every copy line below is a draft. Before anything goes live it runs through
/antislop, and nothing on the App Store side names another platform.

## Top quick wins (under an hour each)

1. **Play short description, 56 to 78 of 80 characters.**
   - Current: "Competition-style BBQ scoring. Track, rank, and compare."
   - Draft: "Judge every BBQ joint you visit: brisket, ribs, sauce and sides, scored 1 to 9"
   - Why: on Play, the short description is the second-strongest indexed
     field. Its 24 unused characters are wasted search surface, and
     "brisket" and "ribs" appear nowhere in the listing.
2. **iOS: state the price model.** Use either the 170-character Promotional
   Text, which changes without a release, or the first lines of the
   description.
   - Draft: "Your first 5 reviews are free. One $3.99 purchase unlocks unlimited reviews, and reviews you've already saved stay open." (it still needs an antislop pass)
   - Why: the listing says only "Free · In-App Purchases". When a buyer
     can't see the price in advance, the paywall later looks like a bait
     and switch.
3. **Play hi-res icon.** Upload the Burnt Ends icon (512 px).
   - Why: 4.4.3 ships the new launcher icon, but the Play listing still
     shows the old badge, so the store and the home screen don't match.
     BRAND-UPDATE-2026-10-12.md already lists the listing graphics as not
     yet updated. The App Store icon comes from the binary, so it updates
     when iOS 4.4.3 is released.
4. **Play full description cleanup.** It's indexed on Play, so this one counts.
   - Remove "Built by a BBQ family from Kansas City", which breaks the
     no-identity-padding rule.
   - Add the features shipped since it was written: Blind Table, Rematch,
     BBQ Near Me, PDF export, share cards and in-app account deletion.
   - Work in "barbecue", "brisket", "ribs", "pulled pork", "smokehouse" and
     "BBQ joints". The text currently leans on "restaurant", and "BBQ" makes
     up only about 1.3% of the words.

## Bigger levers

- **In-app review prompt (code, both platforms).** No review-request code
  exists anywhere in the codebase. Ask once after the user's 3rd saved
  review, never after the paywall, and stay inside Apple's cap of 3 prompts
  per 365 days. It needs a Capacitor plugin. That means the plugin-registry
  doctrine applies: gradle include, dex and registry must all agree, plus a
  guard test. It ships as a normal 4.4.x release.
- **Store title.** "BBQ Scorecard: Barbecue Judge" (29/30) on both stores.
  On iOS, take "barbecue" and "judge" out of the keyword field and spend
  the 15 freed characters on new words such as pulled, burnt ends, sauce
  and meat. The brand stays first, so this is Joel's brand call.
- **Screenshots.** Joel makes these. Go to 8 shots (Play's maximum, and
  Apple allows 10) with the Burnt Ends logos. Add Blind Table and Rematch,
  the two features no competitor has. Captions are indexed on iOS since
  June 2025, so use keyword words in them ("brisket", "ribs").
- **iOS seller name.** It shows "Joel Muiller" because the Apple account is
  an individual one. To show "Holy Smokes BBQ Co", the account has to
  become an organization account, which needs a D-U-N-S number and so
  depends on the LLC item in the legal exposure register. Next quarter.
- **iOS Accessibility Nutrition Label.** 4.4.3 carries the screen reader
  pass, but the label can only be declared truthfully after a tester runs
  the common tasks with VoiceOver on.
- **Later:** a 15 to 30 second preview video (adds 20 to 40% conversion
  on iOS per the skill's benchmarks), Spanish localization, and Custom
  Product Pages once there's an ad or Episode link to point at them.

## Already right

- **App Store subtitle and keywords.** "Rate, rank & map BBQ joints" (27/30)
  plus the 99-character keyword field. No word repeats across title,
  subtitle and keywords.
- **Screenshot captions** ("SCORE EVERY RESTAURANT", "RATE IT LIKE A
  JUDGE", "THE VERDICT", "BRAGGING RIGHTS"): one message per screen, which
  is what the rubric asks for.
- **Category** is Food & Drink on both. Data safety and App Privacy are
  filled in.

## Limitations

- Search volume, ranking positions and install conversion need paid tools
  (AppTweak, Sensor Tower) or the console stats.
- The Play feature graphic and the iOS promotional text can't be confirmed
  from the public pages.
- With 5+ Play downloads and no ratings, ASO is not the bottleneck yet.
  Ratings and installs are. The quick wins are cheap, so they're still
  worth doing.
