v3.2.0

July 6, 2026

FEATURE
  • New Rewards screen — unlock 22 achievements as you log reviews. Milestones at 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, and 100 reviews. Meat-specific tiers for brisket, ribs, pulled pork, chicken, sausage, burnt ends, turkey, plus a Meat Omnivore badge for trying all of them. Travel tiers for unique cities visited. Quality tiers for hitting 5-star reviews and a perfect 9.0. Engagement tiers for photo-heavy reviews and ones where the whole table scored. Tap "Rewards" in the filter bar to see your progress
  • Export any review to PDF — cover page with full scoring breakdown, notes, and up to 5 photos on their own pages. Tap "Export PDF" on a review's detail screen
  • BBQ Notebook is live — if you also cook, check out our new standalone Notebook app. Cross-promo appears in a one-time launch card, on the review detail screen, and in your profile. Dismiss any prompt and it stays quiet for 30 days
