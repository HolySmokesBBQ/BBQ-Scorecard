CHANGELOG

BBQ Notebook by Holy Smokes BBQ Co · Version History

v2.1.7

July 11, 2026

FEATURE
  • Radial rating dial — the cook rating is now a press-and-hold wheel you rotate around like a temperature knob. Continuous 0.0 to 9.0 instead of picking a single number, with a haptic tick at each integer. Now you can rate your cook as precisely as it deserves — the 8.5 that isn’t quite a 9, the 3.2 that wasn’t quite a fail
  • Full keyboard and stepper accessibility for the rating dial — arrow keys step 0.1, PageUp/PageDown step 1, Home/End jump to bounds

v2.1.6

June 22, 2026

FEATURE
  • Rewards & tiers — brand-new Rewards page with three ladders to climb. Overall pitmaster path (First Light at 1 cook all the way to Eternal Pitmaster at 2,500). Per-meat ladders (Brisket Boss, Pulled Pork Patriarch, Hall of the Yardbird, and dozens more — each meat has its own 9-tier ladder up to 1,000 cooks). Plus 8 behavioral achievements (Note Taker, Recipe Author, Lesson Learner, Thermometer Whisperer, etc.)
  • Unlock celebrations — the save banner turns gold and names your new tier the moment you cross a threshold. No streaks, no points, no leaderboards beyond Cook Compare — just earned titles that stick with you
  • Reach Rewards from the home screen quick actions

v2.1.5

June 22, 2026

FEATURE
  • Bulk CSV export — the Stats page now has Export cooks CSV and Export recipes CSV buttons. One click and your whole cook log (or recipe library) lands on your device as a spreadsheet-ready file for backup or analysis
  • Tips from your past cooks — when you start a new cook of a meat you’ve cooked before, the form surfaces the “What I’d change next time” notes from your last few cooks of that meat. Tap to expand. Closes the loop on your own feedback so you don’t repeat the same mistake
  • Filters stick between sessions — if you filter the cook log to one meat type or tag, the filter survives an app restart instead of resetting to All
  • Recipe search — new search box on the Recipes page matches recipe name, ingredient name, and prep notes
  • Tag autocomplete — the cook tag input now suggests tags you’ve already used as you type

v2.1.4

June 22, 2026

FEATURE FIX
  • Export any cook to PDF — new Export PDF button on every cook’s detail page renders the full cook log (meat, rub, wood, smoker, temps, timestamped notes, photos) into a Letter-size PDF you can save, print, or share
  • Multi-photo import — the Import page now accepts up to 5 photos at once for OCR brands. The first one drives OCR; the rest attach to the cook as bonus shots
  • “Cook this again” — the cook detail page action button formerly named Duplicate is now Cook this again, with the same one-tap duplicate behavior
  • MEATER parser tweak — OCR-truncated custom meat names like “Flat (Bottom Rack” are auto-closed to “Flat (Bottom Rack)” on import

v2.1.3

June 20, 2026

FEATURE
  • More thermometer brands — Combustion, ChefsTemp, ThermoPro, Govee, and Maverick now show up in the Import brand picker alongside MEATER, FireBoard, ThermoWorks, Weber Connect, and Inkbird
  • Remembers your brand — the Import page now starts on the brand you last used so you don’t have to scroll the picker every time. Tap ← Back from the file picker if you want to switch brands

v2.1.2

June 11, 2026

FEATURE
  • Universal Thermometer Import — the MEATER Import page is now a full multi-brand Import page. Pick your thermometer brand and we walk you through the right export step for that device
  • CSV import for FireBoard, ThermoWorks, and Weber Connect — cleaner than OCR since those apps already give you structured CSV exports. We pull cook duration, peak food temp, average pit temp, and the cook date automatically
  • Screenshot OCR for MEATER (already there) and Inkbird — same flow as before, brand-specific guide
  • Generic CSV fallback — pick “Other / generic CSV” and any thermometer with a CSV export that has a timestamp column + at least one temperature column will work

v2.1.1

June 11, 2026

FEATURE FIX
  • Sign in with email — Google Sign-In is still here, but now there’s an email + password option for anyone who doesn’t want to tie their cook log to a Google account. Forgot-password reset link works too
  • Cook Share Card — new Share button on every cook’s detail page generates a 1080×1920 image card with the cook name, meat, weight, cook temp, finish temp, cook time, rub, wood, smoker, wrap, and rest time on a charcoal Holy Smokes background.   • Replay the walkthrough — new Show app walkthrough again button on your Profile if you skipped the first-launch tour and want to revisit it

v2.1.0

June 10, 2026

FEATURE FIX
  • Import from MEATER — new home-screen button reads a screenshot of your MEATER Cook Summary screen and pre-fills a cook for you. We pull the meat type, finish temp, cook date, and total duration automatically, then show you what we read so you can review and edit anything before tapping save
  • The screenshot itself attaches as the cook’s first photo so MEATER’s temperature graph stays right there in your cook log forever
  • Fields the OCR isn’t sure about get a gold DOUBLE CHECK chip so you know exactly where to look twice
  • Step-by-step in-app guide walks you through screenshotting the right MEATER screen the first time
  • Floating add-cook button — new + button stays pinned to the bottom-right of the home screen so you can start a new cook from anywhere in the cook log, not just the empty state

FEATURE

v2.0.6

June 10, 2026

FEATURE

v2.0.5

June 10, 2026

FIX SECURITY
  • Cook deletions now sync to the cloud — previously deleting a cook only removed it from your device, then the next cloud sync would resurrect it. Same fix for recipes
  • Cook photos now back up to Cloud Storage — before, photos lived only on your device and would silently disappear if you switched devices or cleared app data. Now they upload on save and stay with your account
  • Photo cleanup — when you delete a cook, its photos are deleted from Cloud Storage too, not just from your device
  • Internal: registered cooks/{userId}/{cookId}/{photo} in the Firebase Storage rules with the same friend-read gating as review photos; tightened the Android-specific gitignore so Firebase credentials can never be committed by accident
