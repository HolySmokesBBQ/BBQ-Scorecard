DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

BBQ Notebook by Holy Smokes BBQ Co

You can delete your data from BBQ Notebook at any time. There are two options depending on what you want to remove.

OPTION 1: DELETE INDIVIDUAL COOKS OR RECIPES

You can delete any cook or recipe directly from within the app:

  1. Open the app and find the cook or recipe you want to delete
  2. Tap to open it
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete
  4. Confirm the deletion

Deleted entries are removed from your device and from Cloud Firestore immediately. Photos attached to deleted cooks are removed from Cloud Storage at the same time. This cannot be undone.

OPTION 2: DELETE YOUR ENTIRE ACCOUNT

To delete your account and all associated data from our servers, send an email to:

datadelete@holysmokesbbqco.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request — BBQ Notebook

Include the email address you used to sign in with Google. We will delete the following data within 7 days:

  1. Your profile — display name, email, photo URL, and friend code
  2. All of your cooks — meat, rub, wood, temps, weather, outcome notes, and any other cook data stored in Cloud Firestore
  3. All of your recipes — saved rubs and sauces
  4. All of your photos — removed from Firebase Cloud Storage
  5. Your friend connections — removed from both your account and your friends' accounts

This is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your cooks, recipes, photos, and friend connections cannot be recovered.

LOCAL DATA

Data stored on your device (localStorage) is not affected by an account deletion request. To clear local data, go to your browser settings and clear site data for holysmokesbbqco.com, or uninstall the app.

WHAT WE KEEP

Nothing tied to your account. When your account is deleted, all data associated with it is permanently removed from Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage. We do not retain backups of deleted user data. Anonymised usage analytics already collected by Google Analytics are held on Google's own retention schedule and are not linked back to a deleted account.

QUESTIONS

If you have questions about data deletion, contact us at datadelete@holysmokesbbqco.com or visit our privacy policy.
