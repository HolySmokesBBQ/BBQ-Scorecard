DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

BBQ Board by Holy Smokes BBQ Co

You can delete your data from BBQ Board at any time. There are two options depending on what you want to remove.

OPTION 1: DELETE INDIVIDUAL PRICE SUBMISSIONS

You can delete any price submission you made directly from within the app. This works for prices you submitted without an account too, as long as you have not signed out on that device:

  1. Open Settings (the ☰ menu, then Settings)
  2. Under Your submissions, find the price
  3. Tap Delete, then tap again to confirm

Deleted submissions are removed from Cloud Firestore immediately. The shop record itself is preserved, since other users may have their own submissions on it.

OPTION 2: DELETE YOUR ENTIRE ACCOUNT

To delete your account and all associated data from our servers, send an email to:

datadelete@holysmokesbbqco.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request: BBQ Board

Include the email address on your account, however you signed in (Google, Apple, or email and password). We will confirm by email and delete the following data without undue delay, and within 30 days at most:

  1. Your sign-in record: the Firebase Authentication account itself (email, sign-in provider and user ID), plus the display name and photo URL it stores
  2. All of your price submissions: every price you've contributed, along with any notes you added

This is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your submissions cannot be recovered.

SHOP RECORDS YOU ADDED

If you were the first user to add a shop to Board, that shop record itself is not deleted when your account is. The shop belongs to the community directory once other users have submitted prices for it. Your name is removed from the record; the shop remains.

IF YOU SUBMITTED PRICES WITHOUT SIGNING IN

There is no account to delete. To have a price you submitted removed, email datadelete@holysmokesbbqco.com with the shop, the cut, the price and roughly when you submitted it, and we will take it down without undue delay, and within 30 days at most.

LOCAL DATA

Data stored on your device (localStorage) is not affected by an account deletion request. To clear local data, go to your browser settings and clear site data for holysmokesbbqco.com, or uninstall the app.

WHAT WE KEEP

Nothing tied to you. When your account is deleted, all data associated with it is permanently removed from Cloud Firestore. We do not retain backups of deleted user data. Shop records that survive have your submitter ID stripped.

QUESTIONS

If you have questions about data deletion, contact us at datadelete@holysmokesbbqco.com or visit our privacy policy.
