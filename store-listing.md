# BBQ Scorecard — Play Store Listing

## Title
BBQ Scorecard

## Short Description
Score BBQ restaurants. Track every visit. Compare with friends.

## Full Description
BBQ Scorecard is a restaurant scoring app built around the KCBS competition judging format. Rate BBQ joints across 10 categories — appearance, taste, tenderness, smoke, sides, sauce, portions, service, cleanliness, and family amenities — on a 1 to 9 scale.

Each review produces a composite score with star ratings. The seven BBQ categories generate a base score, and the three family categories add a bonus. You get an honest, repeatable number for every place you eat.

What you can do with it:

- Score restaurants on 10 categories with built-in descriptors for each level
- Attach photos from your meal (up to 6 per review)
- Run side-by-side comparisons between any two restaurants
- Track which cuts of meat are best at which spots with the Meat MVP tracker
- Filter reviews by trip name or city
- View all your reviewed restaurants on a map
- Generate a Google Review draft from your scores with one tap
- Export your data as CSV for spreadsheets or backup as JSON
- Back up and sync reviews through Google Drive
- Add friends by code and see a shared leaderboard
- See head-to-head scores when friends review the same restaurant

The scoring system gives you a real number, not a gut feeling. A 7.24 means something different than a 6.50, and you can see exactly which categories pulled a place up or dragged it down.

Built by a BBQ family from Kansas City. We started tracking our restaurant visits on the road and needed something better than a notes app. The smoker at home is ours — nobody else touches it — and the scores on here are honest.

Works offline. Your reviews save locally and sync when you're back online.
